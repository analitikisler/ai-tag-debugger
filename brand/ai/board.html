Rules in short

  • Lowercase, always. ai tag debugger, also at the start of a sentence. Never “AI Tag Debugger”.
  • Names are not translated. Turkish adds an apostrophe suffix: ai tag debugger’ı, ai visibility’yi, ai cloud’da.
  • One red point per logo. No product colours; the old orange, teal and blue are retired.
  • “by analitik işler” is the master logo, with its red point and three bars, never the name in type.
  • Clear space 2 S (S = the i's stem) on every side; mark x-height at least 9 px.
  • Status is a word beside the logo, in pencil: Early access, Alpha. Never inside the file.
  • Same height in a row. Lockups that sit together share one height.
  • The red pen marks real findings only: a tick, a circle, an underline. Two per screen at most.
  • No second font. Schibsted lives inside the logo files; UIs use the Analitik İşler system fonts.
